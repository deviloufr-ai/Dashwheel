Dashwheel replaces the home screen of an Android car head unit. Map, music and live car data share one calm screen, and you arrange it yourself.

Made for 1280x720 head units (ROCO K706, FYT and QF units). It also works on upright screens and on phones. Android 10 or newer. No root needed.

DASHBOARDS
• Up to seven dashboards in a cross: three side by side, two above and two below the middle one
• See them all at once, add one with +, remove one with −
• A free grid on each: hold a tile to move it, drag its corner to resize, undo any change
• Ready-made templates: Daily, Road trip and Car health
• 30+ built-in widgets with 60+ designs, plus Android widgets and app shortcuts
• 14 themes, four of them whole-design skins, each with a day and a night version

YOUR CAR
• Live data from a Bluetooth ELM327 OBD adapter: speed, revs, temperatures, fuel, voltage, fault codes read and cleared
• The head unit's own car data on units that share it: doors, fuel level, lights, mileage, tyre pressures
• Car care: particle filter health, engine warm-up, battery, servicing planner with spoken reminders
• Fuel log, LPG tank estimate, parking timer and where you parked

ON THE ROAD
• A free map with search and routes, with turn-by-turn guidance of its own or handed to your navigation app
• Next turn from Google Maps or Waze on its own tile
• Break reminder, eco-driving score, speed limits, fuel prices nearby (France)
• Volume that follows speed, spoken alerts and a start-up briefing
• Your own pop-ups for calls and car events, and steering-wheel buttons you assign yourself

OPTIONAL EXTRAS
• AI mechanic: fault codes explained in plain words with Google Gemini (your own free API key)
• Phone link with the Dashwheel Companion app: notifications, messages and calls on the big screen, paired by QR code and end-to-end encrypted
• Split screen and typing from your phone through Android's accessibility service, which you turn on yourself and can turn off at any time

9 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian.

Free and open source under the GPL v3. No ads, no tracking, no account.

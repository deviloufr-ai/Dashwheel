<p>Dashwheel replaces the home screen of an Android car head unit. Map, music and live car data share one calm screen, and you arrange it yourself.</p>

<p>Made for 1280x720 head units (ROCO K706, FYT and QF units). It also works on upright Tesla-style screens and on phones. Android 10 or newer. No root needed.</p>

<p><b>Dashboards</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Seven dashboards in a cross: three side by side, two above and two below the middle one</li>
<li>A free grid on each: hold a tile to move it, drag its corner to resize, undo any change</li>
<li>Ready-made templates: Daily, Road trip and Car health</li>
<li>30+ built-in widgets with 60+ designs, plus Android widgets and app shortcuts</li>
<li>14 themes, four of them whole-design skins (Orbit, Cockpit, Horizon, Tape Deck), each with a day and a night version</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Your car</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Live data from a Bluetooth ELM327 OBD adapter: speed, revs, temperatures, fuel, voltage, fault codes read and cleared</li>
<li>The head unit's own car data on units that share it: doors, fuel level, lights, mileage, tyre pressures</li>
<li>Car care: particle filter health, engine warm-up, battery, servicing planner with spoken reminders</li>
<li>Fuel log, LPG tank estimate, parking timer and where you parked</li>
</ul>

<p><b>On the road</b></p>
<ul>
<li>A free MapLibre map with search and routes, with turn-by-turn guidance of its own or handed to your navigation app</li>
<li>Next turn from Google Maps or Waze on its own tile</li>
<li>Break reminder, eco-driving score, speed limits, fuel prices nearby (France)</li>
<li>Volume that follows speed, spoken alerts and a start-up briefing</li>
<li>Your own pop-ups for calls and car events, and steering-wheel buttons you assign yourself</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Optional extras</b></p>
<ul>
<li>AI mechanic: fault codes explained in plain words with Google Gemini (your own free API key)</li>
<li>Phone link with the Dashwheel Companion app: notifications, messages and calls on the big screen, paired by QR code and end-to-end encrypted on your phone's hotspot</li>
<li>Split screen and typing from your phone through Android's accessibility service, which you turn on yourself and can turn off at any time</li>
</ul>

<p>9 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian.</p>

<p>Free and open source under the GPL v3. No ads, no tracking, no account. Updates come from Google Play.</p>

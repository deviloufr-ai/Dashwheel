# Dashwheel review, 2026-10-10

What this review covers: widgets, UX/UI and skins, code (speed, smoothness, RAM, structure), new features, and a comparison with other car launchers.

How it was done: by reading the code only. Nothing was built, measured or run on the unit, so the costs below are estimates. Paths are under `app/src/main/java/com/openauto/dash/` unless a full path is given. Items that the 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-07 reviews already list are not repeated.

---

## 1. Where Dashwheel stands against other launchers

### What the others are like

- **AGAMA:** 5M+ installs, rated 4.5. Buttons, a speedometer and a media "info circle". It added TPMS and widgets that change with the time of day in v5.1.1. It can't control the MCU volume, and Waze doesn't work in its navigation widget.
- **CarWebGuru:** 1M+ installs, rated 3.4. 40+ themes and a theme builder, desktops you can import and export, GPS tracks, a trip calendar, OBD widgets and an automation API. Users find it confusing and dated.
- **FCC:** setup profiles per chipset, a parked screensaver, steering-wheel keys, a mini-map. No update since 2023.
- **Car Launcher Pro:** $15.99. The best trip statistics: per trip, day, week and month, plus 0-100 and quarter-mile times.
- **AutoZen:** a launcher that runs on the phone. Speed cameras and auto-start on Bluetooth are paid.
- **Headunit Reloaded:** runs Android Auto on the head unit. Rated 2.1.
- **Torque Pro, Car Scanner, OBD Fusion:** OBD dashboards. None of them explains a fault in plain words.
- **Car makers:**
  - CarPlay Ultra puts branded gauge themes in the instrument cluster.
  - iOS 26 and Android Auto 2026 put 2-3 widgets next to the map.
  - Gemini, MBUX and Tesla Grok are assistants that can change the route and car settings.
  - The on-screen 3D car mirrors the real one (doors, lights).
  - Tesla's dashcam shows speed, gear and steering over the video.

**Where Dashwheel leads.** Nobody else has live apps in tiles, a talking AI mechanic, CAN reverse lines and radar, owner tools (fuel, LPG, servicing), the phone companion or a Pi cluster. Dashwheel is closer to a car maker's system than to an aftermarket launcher.

**Where it lags:**
- Trip statistics (Car Launcher Pro)
- Sharing a single dashboard or skin (CarWebGuru)
- Automation hooks (CarWebGuru)
- Widgets that change with context (AGAMA, iOS and Android Auto widget stacks)
- Polish: every skin redraws only 7 of the 56 widgets (section 3)

## 2. Widgets

There are 56 widget kinds (`DashboardModel.kt:35-103`); the add sheet offers 51. There are 67 designs: Standard, 23 generic designs and 43 made for one widget only.

Calendar, 0-100 timer, eco score, tyres, sunrise/sunset, altitude, messages, quick dial and voice notes all already exist.

### New widgets, ranked by value for effort

| # | Widget | Built from | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fuel economy**: instant and average L/100, cost of this drive | `CarBody.instantConsumption` (`CarBoxData.kt:28`, today only a text row at `CarCareTiles.kt:549`), FuelLog's real L/100, fuel prices. The demo already fakes it (`DemoMode.kt:407`). | S |
| 2 | **Climate**: temperatures, fan, A/C, recirculation | `CarBox.climate` (`CarBox.kt:60`) and the drawing in `ClimateOverlay.kt`; needs a climate `demoWrite` | S |

| 10 | **Border rules**, spoken on entering a country: limits, vignette, daytime lights | Nominatim (`WeatherAlerts.kt:116`) + a fixed table | S |

| 12 | **Home arrival action**: a garage/gate/Home Assistant webhook fired near home, or by a wheel button | Places, GPS, phone data | M |

Left out on purpose:
- EV chargers: the car is a diesel.
- Dashcam: the only camera is the reverse camera.
- Lyrics and visualiser: distracting at the wheel.
- Toll cost and traffic: no free, reliable source.

### Existing widgets to upgrade

- **Trip computer:** GPS only, one trip (`DriveTiles.kt:346`, `LiveFeeds.kt:50`). Add an automatic trip A per drive and a trip B since the last fill-up (from FuelLog), with litres and cost. This closes the gap with Car Launcher Pro.
- **Eco driving:** the cost uses the profile's *guessed* consumption (`CarCareTiles.kt:349`), even though FuelLog's real figure and the car box's instant figure exist.
- **Battery:** add a sparkline, resting voltage and the cranking dip. `BatteryWatch` (`VehicleAlerts.kt:142`) already tracks part of this.
- **Agenda:** add a "leave by" time using Home and work's routing.
- **Voice notes:** tap only. Add a wheel action, transcription and send to phone (hands-free first).
- **Music player:** add a "next source" wheel action (FM, Bluetooth, Spotify).
- **Performance timer:** add 80-120 km/h, which is more useful than 0-100 in a 110 hp MPV.
- **Demo mode:** these show nothing when parked in demo:
  - Speed limit, Speed cameras, Weather alerts
  - Home and work, Parking timer, GPS satellites
  - Climate, radar
  - FuelLog, Maintenance, LpgTank

## 3. UX/UI and skins

### Skin coverage

`SKINNED_KINDS` (`Skins.kt:50-58`) lists only 7 widgets that a skin redraws: Telemetry, Speed, Media, Navigation, Clock, Weather and Range. The other 49 draw their Standard face **with no panel**, straight on the skin's background. So Fault codes or Tyres end up as bare text over Racing's road or Anime's sky. On the default three-page layout, only 5 of 10 widgets wear the skin.

| Skin | Own widgets | Top bar / Maps frame | Speed colour from the limit | Upright-screen code | Car render | Own alert finish |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orbit, Cockpit, Horizon, Tape Deck | 7/56 | yes / yes | no (fixed 110) | yes | no | yes |
| Canvas | 7/56 | rail / none | no | yes | no | yes |
| Futuristic, Motorsport, Anime | 7/56 | yes / yes | no | **none found** | yes | borrowed |
| Simple, Modern | 7/56 | yes / yes | **yes** | **none found** | yes | borrowed |
| Racing | 7/56 | yes / yes | no | yes | yes | borrowed |

### P0: safety while driving

1. **Background animation keeps running while the car moves.**
   - Effects default to FULL (`DashTheme.kt:523`). The ambient ticker (`SkinKit.kt:158`, `rememberLoop`) runs at 20 fps whatever the speed.
   - Animated backgrounds: Orbit, Futuristic hologram, Horizon, Modern city, Racing road, Anime petals, Tape Deck, Simple, Motorsport.
   - The Anime car animates *more* when moving (`AnimeSkinArt.kt:186`).
   - **Fix:** in `ambientStepMs`, return a still frame while `LocalDriveLock.current.moving`. Status pulses and needles keep moving. The wow stays at a standstill and when parked, which is where it gets looked at anyway.
2. **The speed warning loses contrast exactly when it matters.** Anime at night draws a speed of 110 or more as white on amber, about 2.1:1 (`AnimeSkinArt.kt:90-92`). **Fix:** dark ink on a warning fill, and check every skin's warning state reaches at least 4.5:1.

### P1

3. **Give the 49 fallback tiles the skin's panel.**
   - Add a `SkinSurface` to SkinKit: panel, title and number styles, accent and status lamp. Wrap every fallback tile in it. That is cheap and gives one look per page.
   - Then redraw the widgets on the default layout first: Audio, Notifications, Fault codes, Servicing, Battery, Quick dial, Home/Work.
4. **Touch targets are phone-sized.**
   - Today (`DashTokens.kt:40-45`): `Touch` 48 dp, `TouchPrimary` 56 dp. The new skins' media buttons go down to 48 dp (`FuturisticSkinTiles.kt:827`, `AnimeSkinTiles.kt:647`).
   - The Android Automotive guideline is 76 dp for anything used while driving.
   - **Fix:** `TouchPrimary` = 76 dp (play/pause, call, answer), `Touch` = 56 dp, 12 dp between targets. The drawing can stay smaller inside a bigger hit area.
5. **Text below the 14 sp floor in the new skins:**
   - Racing bar: 10-12 sp (`RacingSkin.kt:1086-1108`)
   - Modern: 12-13 sp (`ModernSkinTiles.kt:338, 1226`)
   - Futuristic: 13 sp (`FuturisticSkin.kt:680`)
   - Motorsport: 13 sp (`MotorsportSkin.kt:479`)

   **Fix:** a `SKIN_MIN_SP` floor in each skin's text helper; drop the line rather than shrink it.
6. **Day accents fail in sunlight:**
   - Anime: 2.7:1 (`AnimeSkin.kt:117`)
   - Motorsport: 3.6:1 (`MotorsportSkin.kt:110`)
   - Racing: 4.0:1 (`RacingSkin.kt:135`)

   **Fix:** keep the accent for fills and add a darker `AccentInk` for text, with a contrast test next to `DashThemeTest.kt`.
7. **The speed colour means different things by skin.** Nine skins turn amber at a fixed 110 (`TelemetryTiles.kt:98`); only Modern and Simple follow the real speed limit. **Fix:** one shared `speedState(speed, limit)` in SkinKit.
8. **Turn arrows guessed from text (still open).** Cockpit (`CockpitSkin.kt:1716`) and Motorsport (`MotorsportSkinTiles.kt:716`) match parts of words, so "Brighton Rd" reads as "right". None of the skins reads `NavState.maneuver`. **Fix:** one shared `turnOf(nav)`: maneuver first, then a whole-word match.
9. **Navigate home and calling a favourite take 3 or more taps on the default layout** (`DashTemplates.kt:64-66`). **Fix:**
   - Put a Home/Work and Quick dial strip on the daily Home page.
   - Pre-map the unit's named keys: Phone → read or answer, Navi → navigate home.
10. **The Canvas weather shows "--°" forever and never says how old it is** (`CanvasSkin.kt:486`). **Fix:** use `rememberWeatherWait` like the other skins.

### Missing UX patterns

| Pattern | Today | Proposal |
|---|---|---|
| Media always reachable | Bar items are readouts only (`BarItems.kt:93`) | A MEDIA bar item: what's playing plus play/pause at 76 dp |
| Quick settings / notification shade | Wheel action only, needs accessibility | Pull down from the bar: Wi-Fi, BT, brightness, Quiet, recent messages |
| Switching dashboards with context | PARKED, DRIVING and ENGINE_FAULT only (`CanvasTabs.kt:120`) | Add navigation started → map page, ignition on → Home, low fuel → fuel page. Turn PARKED on by default. |
| Smart stack | Missing | Widget #8 above |

### P2: design-system debt

- **Literal sizes and colours.** The six new skins use 134-212 dp literals and 42-121 `Color(0x` literals each, with almost no `DashSize`, `DashSpace` or `DashShape` tokens. Anime runs a second palette (`AnimeTones`), so skinned and fallback tiles on the same page don't match.
- **Pieces copied between skins that belong in SkinKit:** `MediaProgress`, `AppGlyph`, `fitSp`, the "fits on one line" measurer (5 copies), `FuelBar`, `ObdLamp`, `BarClock`.
- **Thresholds written as literals.** Coolant 105 appears in 8 places, plus 110 and 115; Motorsport's redline fallback is 4500 against `SKIN_RPM_MAX` 7000. **Fix:** add `SKIN_COOLANT_WARN_C` and `SKIN_COOLANT_CRIT_C`.
- **Skin corners ignored:** `Card` always uses `DashShape.Large`, and the edit outline is 24 dp even on Racing's 2 dp tiles. **Fix:** use `skinChrome().shapes`.
- **Alerts:** the six new skins borrow an older finish (`AlertChrome.kt:78`). The Anime speech bubble and the Motorsport flag frame from the mockups never reached the alerts.
- **Setup:** first-run setup offers only Auto, Cockpit and Orbit (`SetupScreen.kt:534`), so the six skins with a car render are never seen there.
- **Upright screen:** the six new skins have no upright-specific code. They need a 720x1280 pass.
- **Italic speed digits** (Racing, Anime) read slower at a glance. Keep the speed upright and use italic for labels only.

### New skin ideas

Each would need a hero render, depth, and motion only when parked.

1. **Citroën "Picasso" native.** Echo the C4 Picasso's own centre cluster: big centre digital speed, segmented bars, the white/blue backlight choice, the car on a glass band. Nobody else ships this, and it is your car.
2. **VFD / 80s.** Cyan-green fluorescent segments with glow, in the spirit of the BX and CX. High contrast and naturally still while driving.
3. **Map first (Tesla-like).** The car on the left with live doors and tyres, the map filling the rest. Also the calmest look to drive with.
4. **Glass slab (MBUX-like).** Ambient gradient tied to speed or temperature, a layer of context cards.
5. **Curved cockpit (iDrive-like).** Panels tilted toward the driver, using `driverOnRight`.
6. **Calm Scandinavian (Polestar-like).** Large type, 76 dp targets, muted colours. A reference for the touch-size fix.
7. **OLED pure black** for night and **e-ink paper** for direct sun.

## 4. Code: speed, smoothness, RAM, structure

The code is already tuned in many places: draw caches in the skins, tickers that stop on hidden pages, apps loaded off the main thread, deferred starts, sampled photo decodes. These are the gaps that remain. **Every item below is invisible to the user** (house rule for performance work), except where noted.

| # | Finding | Evidence | Gain | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tile text reading runs nonstop on the main thread.** Text recognition (ML Kit) every 1.5 s whenever Waze, or Maps that isn't guiding, is in a tile. `TextureView.getBitmap` reads back from the GPU on the UI thread and allocates a new ~1.6 MB bitmap each time. | `TileText.kt:43` (`Dispatchers.Main.immediate`), `EmbeddedApp.kt:237` | CPU 15-30% of one core, GC jank during swipes | S-M |
| 2 | **Charging and Gemini pulses redraw the window at 60 fps.** `rememberInfiniteTransition` is in every skin's bar while the phone charges, even with effects off. The Gemini pulse also recomposes every frame. | `TopBar.kt:325, 869`, `GeminiLive.kt:531, 560` | GPU+CPU for the whole drive | S |
| 3 | **My car look keeps every picture at 1400 px in ARGB, even when off.** | `MyCarLook.kt:97, 150, 390-406` | RAM ~20-30 MB | M |
| 4 | **The whole dashboard recomposes every minute and in the middle of each swipe.** `AutomotiveDashboard()` is about 2,100 lines with 87 states; the clock and `currentPage` are read at the root, and `TopBarModel` gets ~20 new lambdas each time. | `AutomotiveDashboard.kt:936, 1167-1245` | Jank | M |
| 5 | **Tiles recompose on every GPS fix.** Weather (28 places), fuel prices, home/work and widget faces collect the full `Location` at 1 Hz. **Fix:** `hasFix` and ~1 km-cell flows. | `SkinKit.kt:266`, `LiveFeeds.kt:114` | CPU | S |
| 6 | **Slow readouts follow every OBD sample.** `rememberFuel(obdData)`, the bar's fuel/coolant/battery and the battery face take the full `ObdData`. **Fix:** per-field `derivedStateOf`, as in `CarCareTiles.kt:145`. | `SkinKit.kt:295`, `BarItems.kt:243` | CPU at 2-5 Hz | S |
| 7 | **Map tiles on hidden neighbouring pages keep drawing at full rate.** The wallpaper is the only map with an fps cap. **Fix:** `setMaximumFps(1)` when off screen (not `onPause`, to avoid the black-on-wake bug). | `MapLibrePanel.kt:162` | GPU | S |
| 8 | **Baseline profile is a hand-written wildcard; the shipped profile is only 7 KB.** It either compiles everything or almost nothing; measure it first. **Fix:** a Macrobenchmark module on the K706 emulator AVD and a generated profile. | `app/src/main/baseline-prof.txt`, `CompileAfterUpdate.kt:43` | Startup, RAM | M |
| 9 | **Startup reads settings on the main thread.** Five settings files are read before the first frame, there are 61 settings files in all, and 29 subsystems start in one main-thread task. **Fix:** load them in parallel early, spread the starts over idle time. | `MainActivity.kt:145-212` | Cold start | S |
| 10 | **Thread hygiene:** <br>• Gemini Live checks for root on the main thread (spawns `su`, 8 s timeout; a "no" is never remembered). <br>• The OBD reply wait sleeps 5 ms at a time (~200 wake-ups a second). <br>• ParkingMemory runs a 10 Hz loop even with nothing parked. <br>• DebugLog writes synchronously. <br>• Polling in `InfoTiles.kt:958` and `NetworkLists.kt:377` ignores `LocalPageActive`. | `GeminiLive.kt:115, 203`, `SystemInstaller.kt:42`, `ObdBluetoothManager.kt:1091`, `ParkingMemory.kt:63`, `DebugLog.kt:64` | UI freezes on non-root units, CPU | S |
| 11 | **No Compose stability settings.** `MediaState` holds a `Bitmap`; pages are passed as a `List`. **Fix:** a stability config file plus compiler reports. | `CarMediaController.kt:21` | Recomposition | S |
| 12 | **The shrinker keeps all of MapLibre** (`-keep org.maplibre.** { *; }`, 8.7 MB of code). | `proguard-rules.pro` | APK size and code RAM; needs a navigation test drive | M |

### Structure

- **One package.** All 252 app files sit in `com.openauto.dash`.
- **The dashboard function.** Split `AutomotiveDashboard` into:
  - a `DashboardState` class (states and actions, testable on the JVM)
  - `DashboardPager`
  - `LauncherBar`
  - `DashboardSheets`
  - `EditModeController`
- **Skins.** The 11 looks total about 26k lines, and each re-implements Clock, Media, Range, Speed, Telemetry, Weather, the launch bar and the top bar, data side included. Move per-widget data models (`RangeModel`, `SpeedModel`, …) into SkinKit so skins only draw. This also fixes #5 and #6 once, and makes the "49 fallback tiles" work in section 3 much cheaper.
- **Gradle modules:**
  - `:core:car` (OBD, CarBox, MCU, power, signals)
  - `:core:shell` (root and system displays)
  - `:feature:embedded` (EmbeddedApp, SystemDisplayHelper, TileText)
  - `:feature:map` (MapLibre and navigation)
  - `:ui:skins`
  - `:feature:settings`

  This also speeds up incremental builds.
- **Lifetimes.** 219 `object`s, 133 `MutableStateFlow`s and 77 scopes that are never cancelled. A small feed registry that starts and stops feeds with ignition and visibility would make their lifetimes clear.
- **Settings keys.** Raw string keys across 65 files. A typed `PrefKey<T>` would remove typo bugs.
- **Tests.** There are 97 JVM tests, but no screenshot tests and no benchmarks. Per-skin screenshot tests (Roborazzi) are the cheapest proof that a performance change has no visible effect.

## 5. New feature proposals

Ranked for this car and the hands-free rule.

1. **Context-aware dashboards.** Extend the PARKED/DRIVING triggers:
   - navigation started → map page
   - ignition on → Home
   - low fuel → fuel page
   - reversing → the camera (already does)

   Add the smart stack (widget #8). This is the main trend in AGAMA, iOS 26 and Android Auto 2026, and it removes taps.
2. **Trip A/B and drive history on the unit.** Per drive and since fill-up: litres, cost, average L/100, eco score. Car Launcher Pro's strongest feature, and the data is already in DriveLog and FuelLog.
3. **Assistant that acts.** Gemini Live already knows the car facts (DashAssistant, CarFacts). Give it tools such as:
   - "add a fuel stop on the way"
   - "navigate home"
   - "call Marie"
   - "is the particle filter due?"
   - "start the parking timer"

   This is where MBUX, Tesla Grok and Gemini in Android Auto are heading.
4. **ZFE / Crit'Air and border alerts**, spoken (widgets #6 and #10). France-specific value no other launcher has.
5. **Cluster gauge themes on the Pi.** The Pi already draws the map. Offer three or four cluster faces matching the skins (CarPlay Ultra's idea). The Pi is the only aftermarket "cluster" around.
6. **Drive replay.** Replay a drive from DriveLog on the map with speed, rpm, gear and the eco score over it, like Tesla's dashcam overlay but without a camera. Good for "why was that drive so costly?".
7. **Share one dashboard or skin.** Export a single dashboard (layout, designs, skin) as a file or a QR code for the companion or the wiki. SetupBackup already serialises everything. CarWebGuru's community lives on this.
8. **Automation intents.** A small documented broadcast/intent API so Tasker or MacroDroid can switch dashboards, speak text or read car values (CarWebGuru has one). Cheap and loved by head-unit tinkerers.
9. **Voice notes by wheel → phone.** Hold a button, speak, get a transcript in the companion's agenda.
10. **Android Auto inside a tile** (via a Headunit Revived-style receiver). Users still ask for projection, but it is high effort. Keep it on the long list.

## 6. Suggested order

1. **Safety and polish, small changes:**
   - background animation still while moving (P0 1)
   - warning contrast (P0 2)
   - 76 dp touch tokens (P1 4)
   - 14 sp floor (P1 5)

   - text reading (#1)
   - pulses (#2)
   - GPS and OBD derived state (#5, #6)
   - hidden map fps (#7)
   - Gemini `su` call off the main thread (#10)
   - startup settings (#9)
3. **SkinSurface for the 49 fallback tiles**, then per-widget data models in SkinKit (UX P1 3 + structure).
4. **Quick widgets:** fuel economy, climate, tell-tales, drive history, trip A/B.
5. **Context-aware dashboards + smart stack**, then the assistant with tools.
6. **Citroën native skin** (mockup first, per the "wow" rule).
7. **Structure:** `DashboardState` extraction behind skin screenshot tests, Macrobenchmark + baseline profile, Gradle modules.

## 7. Done on 2026-10-10 (branch `review-2026-10-10`, emulator only)

1. **Safety and polish:**
   - Background motion holds still while moving (`LocalCarMoving`).
   - Readable speed warning in Anime.
   - One speed colour rule for every look: `speedOver` follows the road's limit when known.
   - One turn rule (`NavState.turnSide`, maneuver first, whole words).
   - 14 sp floor in the new skins.
   - `AccentInk` for accent-coloured words, so every theme reaches 4.5:1 (tested).
   - 76 dp touch areas on the dashboard, without moving a pixel (`carTouchConfiguration`).
   - Canvas weather says why it is empty.
2. **Performance, invisible:**
   - Tile text reader: reuses one bitmap, skips mid-swipe and unchanged frames, reads off the main thread.
   - Pulses at the status rate, read while drawing.
   - Tiles follow coarse GPS cells and `hasFix`, and only their own OBD fields.
   - Hidden maps at 10 fps.
   - Gemini Live no longer probes `su` on a tap.
   - Parking tick only during a manoeuvre.
   - Debug log written off the caller's thread.
   - Network list polls stop on hidden pages.
   - Startup starts one main-thread message at a time.
3. **Skins:** every widget a skin does not redraw sits on that skin's own panel (`SkinFallbackPanel`).
4. **Widgets:**
   - Trip computer with "This drive" and "Since fill-up", each with fuel and cost.
   - New Drive history widget.
   - Tile titles in caps everywhere.
5. **Context:** dashboards can also come up when navigation starts or fuel gets low.

**Not done:**
- Default layout (Home/Work and Quick dial on Home): your call.
- OBD blocking reader: low gain, risky.
- Settings preload.
- Smart stack.
- Assistant tools.
- Citroën skin (mockup first).
- Structure work: `DashboardState`, modules, Macrobenchmark, baseline profile.

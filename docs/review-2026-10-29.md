# Code and usability review, 2026-10-29

A whole-code pass with three questions: where is the code wrong, where does it
work harder than it needs to, and where does the app stop making sense to a
driver. Seven readers took one area each (the dashboard runtime, vehicle data
and local I/O, the network and the AI, the phone companion with the link and
the second screen, embedded apps and the shell, the skins and widget library,
the user-facing flows); every finding below was then checked against the code
again before it was fixed or listed. Nothing was built or run on a unit:
Android's SDK could not be fetched from the review machine, so the fixes were
compiled only as far as a bare Kotlin compiler goes (syntax, imports, the pure
helpers and their tests). CI runs lint and the unit tests on this branch; the
items marked "car" below want a drive before they are trusted.

Items the 2026-10-03, 10-07, 10-10 and 10-22 reviews list are not repeated
unless they were found still open. Paths are under
`app/src/main/java/com/openauto/dash/` unless written in full.

## Found, not changed

Each needs a car, a measurement or a design choice first.

- **Map wallpaper as a SurfaceView** (perf review 2026-10-22, item 2) and the **reverse camera's second capture target** (item 37): the two biggest remaining wins, both need the K706.
- **OBD `sendCommand` spins on `available()`** (item 9): a reader thread with a queue; the link has bitten before.
- **Root recomposition cascades into every tile**: the per-page callbacks close over local `fun`s in `AutomotiveDashboard`, so they are new objects on every root recomposition (once a minute, every swipe) and `DashboardPage` is never skipped; `SkinTileEnv` is rebuilt per tile. A `remember`ed callback bundle fixes it; verify with Compose compiler reports.
- **One ticker per skin caller** (item 34), **PipAnchor's per-tile polling** (item 35).
- **`WheelMonitor.sniff` runs raw `su -c`** without the shell session's pid tracking: a root `getevent` lingers after stop until the next key press. Route it through `RootShell`.
- **`ShizukuShellService` kills only `sh` on a timeout**, not the command's children; a `killTree` as `RootShell` has.
- **Road watch's `SignFinder` allocates ~3.7 MB of scratch arrays per call** (every third frame, every frame for 1.5 s after a sign): a reusable scratch per thread. `Luma.fromRgba` and the detectors read three bytes per pixel where one `getInt` would do.
- **Each H.264 access unit is copied three times on the head unit and four on the Pi** before the decoder: offset-carrying buffers in `VideoPacket`/`SecureChannel`/`GstVideoSink`.
- **The Trend chart rebuilds paths, brushes and labels per draw** (every 10 s only): keep them in the cache scope.
- **The alert preview's caller is a French number in all ten languages** (`AlertStyles.kt`): a string per language.
- **The quick-dial "call back" offer and the agenda's "ongoing" read the clock in composition** with nothing to recompose them: `rememberWallClock`.
- **Fuel and range learned mappings decode on every repeated frame** (`McuReader`): left as is, so a mapping learned while the frame is unchanged still publishes.
- **`DashAssistant` sends the Gemini key in the WebSocket URL** where the REST client puts it in a header; the Live API's header support wants a device test before the change.

## Usability

The usability pass found the earlier reviews' asks done (settings regrouped with search, Quiet in the menu, setup Back stepping back, update no longer stuck, confirmations on Forget and Erase, long press to arrange, companion pair-first and saveable text). What remains, ranked. Nothing here was changed in code.

### Outright usability bugs

1. **Buttons that do nothing on a unit without a browser**: `launchSafely` returns false and every caller ignores it (About › Ko-fi, Project page, Play listing; Report › Open here; the crash offer; the wiki's "Open on this screen"). Many units have no browser. Say "No browser on this unit: scan the code with your phone" (the QR is beside each).
2. **The developer's car in everyone's fault advice**: `vehicle_dtc_fallback_fix` tells every driver to search "… Citroën C4 Picasso". Use the profile's make and model.
3. **Settings search misses settings that exist**: Report how it works, Beta updates, Connect by itself, Save the unit's log, Deep OBD, the Road watch and Gemini Live alert rows, the bar readouts, Automation are not in `SETTINGS_INDEX`. Index every row title `searchTarget()` marks.
4. **A group's scroll position is lost after any deep sheet** (open since 10-03): Back from "Steering wheel buttons" lands at the top of Driving. One `ScrollState` per tab, kept.
5. **Likely truncation on upright screens**: the 12 category tabs in rows of four at one line (German, French), and the Alerts grid's two 112 dp pill columns leaving 70-100 dp for the alert's name. Stack or shorten below `NARROW_SETTINGS`.
6. **A download cannot be cancelled** and the update row is a dead tap while it runs: on a phone hotspot that is paid data, for up to 30 min. `downloadManager.remove`, shown on the row and the overview card.
7. **Companion: Back on Drives, Road watch or Tools leaves the app**; it should return Home first.
8. **Companion: the setup checklist re-sorts under the finger** as permissions come back granted. Fixed order, done rows marked.

### Quick wins

- **One-tap data loss without confirm or Undo**: OBD adapter Forget, PID explorer Forget all, Sensor watch forget, USB TPMS forget, Trip Reset, G-force Reset peaks, parking spot Clear, crash offer Delete. The `ConfirmDialog` and the 5 s Undo strip already exist.
- **Tappables under 48 dp**: the sheet's back arrow (32), the launch-bar editor's up/down/Remove (40, side by side), the Ask-the-mechanic mic (40, used while driving), the empty dashboard's Fill/Add/All (40), the report's Works/Doesn't chips (32), the move-to-dashboard cross cells (24), the settings rail rows (44), the hidden bar's pill (about 33 tall).
- **Raw exception text reaches the driver** in the boot-logo results, "Failed: %1$s" of the PMPatch dialog and the adapter's error on Erase: a short reason and a next step, the detail in the log.
- **"No root (Magisk) and the ADB socket on port %1$d isn't listening"** is jargon with no way out: say the unit can't open apps in windows without root or Shizuku, with "Open full screen" and the Shizuku dialog.
- **Tap sound toggle gives no sample when turned on** (the tap plays before the setting saves).
- **Companion hero "Waiting for the car on your hotspot" has no button**: the hotspot intent exists in the setup panel.
- **"More car alerts need root or a PC command" has no help link**; the **Automation apps** toggle has an invisible effect and no pointer to docs/automation.md; **"Needs the QF firmware"** on other units is a reason nobody can act on (name the units, or hide the row).
- **The compatibility report has no notes field** though `IssueLink.compatibilityBody` takes one.
- Two strings still use arrows for settings paths (`boot_no_adb`, `ai_no_voice`, the companion's `pair_hint`); everywhere else it is "Settings, Group, Row".

### Medium

- **Settings closed by the drive lock are not restored**: creeping forward at a light while typing a key drops the driver on the dashboard with no way back but Overview. Remember the tab and stack; offer "Back to Settings" once parked, as the setup does.
- **The install-permission step is a toast under Android's settings screen**: an in-app dialog first.
- **A frozen app inside a tile has no recovery while driving**: "Restart the app" lives in the tile panel, which needs edit mode, which is parked-only. A long press on the tile, or a ⋮ row.
- **Deep OBD lines are set by cycling on tap** through nine choices: a picker sheet.
- **Companion permissions are still asked as one list**, with background location and battery counted as "left to do": ask when a feature is first used (named as the next step in docs/companion-redesign-2026-10-26.md).
- **Vocabulary**: "Dashwheel Companion" / "phone app" / "companion app" / "the companion"; "tile" vs "widget"; "head unit" / "the unit" / "this unit". `CLAUDE.md` points at a `GLOSSARY.md` that does not exist: one term per concept, the strings checked against it.
- **Ten strings over 230 characters** shown as a dialog body or row detail (`boot_hint`, `boot_no_adb`, `vehicle_finder_help`, `access_a11y_body`, `split_a11y_description`, `lpg_method_engine_detail`, `mycar_photos_hint`, `second_screen_board_no_video`, `shizuku_body`, `tpms_usb_explanation`): one sentence on screen, the rest behind the sheet's "?".
- **Back inside Settings** has no handler of its own: from a group it should step to Overview, then close (to verify on the unit).

### Larger

- **No non-touch navigation**: units with a rotary knob or D-pad (MTC, FYT) cannot walk Settings, the Add sheet or a sheet's buttons; the wheel maps keys to actions, never to move/select. Focusable rows with a visible ring, DPAD/Enter, wheel actions Next / Previous / Select.
- **A real sun theme**: the day palette puts white cards on `F1F3F4` (1.1:1) with 8 % black lines; in glare only the text separates tiles, and most skins are dark-only. A high-contrast Day variant, picked from the light sensor.
- **One rescue place on the unit**: the phone's Rescue card needs the link; on the unit its five fixes (standard look, show the bar, leave split, reset wheel buttons, Home back) live in five places. About › "Something wrong?" with the same five, each with Undo.
